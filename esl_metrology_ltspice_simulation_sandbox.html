Primary Metrological Methodologies for ESL Extraction

In high-power switching topologies (e.g., converters utilizing WBG semiconductors like GaN/SiC), the Equivalent Series Inductance ($ESL$) of the DC-link or snubbing film capacitor governs transient overvoltage spikes, localized EMI generation, and switching loss profiles. Extracting this parasitic inductance in the nanohenry range requires distinct physical approaches across the frequency and time domains.

Below is the mathematical and physical decomposition of all seven distinct methodologies found in our repositories:

1. Auto-Balanced Bridge (ABB)

LCR Meter

Core Governing Physics:

Relies on maintaining a virtual ground potential at the Low-Force ($L_{\text{cur}}$) terminal of the capacitor. An active Transimpedance Operational Amplifier dynamically feeds back current through a range resistor $R_r$ to nullify the potential at $L_{\text{cur}}$.

$$Z_x = -R_r \cdot \frac{V_x}{V_r}$$

Where $V_x$ is the applied voltage and $V_r$ is the voltage across the range resistor. $ESL$ is extracted above the Self-Resonant Frequency (SRF) using:

$$X_{\text{total}} = \omega \cdot ESL - \frac{1}{\omega C} \implies ESL \approx \frac{\text{Im}(Z_x)}{\omega} \quad (\text{at } f \gg \text{SRF})$$
Primary Limitation: Limited to $\approx 120\text{ MHz}$ due to transimpedance amplifier open-loop gain rolloff, resulting in systematic feedback errors.

2. Low Voltage Surge Discharge

TDK/EPCOS

Core Governing Physics:

A charged capacitor is discharged into an ultra-low impedance short circuit loop (using a mercury-wetted or solid-state switch), generating a second-order underdamped $RLC$ transient.

The total loop inductance $L_{\text{total}} = L_d + L_c$ is derived from the period of oscillation $T$ and the peak current decay ratio:

$$\alpha = \frac{1}{T} \ln\left(\frac{I_{p1}}{I_{p2}}\right) \quad \omega_0 = \sqrt{\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}\right)^2 + \alpha^2} \quad L_{\text{total}} = \frac{1}{\omega_0^2 C}$$

To isolate the capacitor's internal ESL ($L_c$) from the discharger fixture inductance ($L_d$), the ratio of the first inductive voltage drop ($V_1$) to initial capacitor charge ($V_c$) is extracted:

$$L_c = L_{\text{total}} \cdot \frac{V_1}{V_c}$$
Primary Limitation: Spark-gap/switch non-linearity, transient contact resistance, and scope channel deskew limit high sensitivity.

3. VNA 2-Port Shunt-Thru

RF VNA

Core Governing Physics:

The gold-standard for milliohm and sub-nanohenry impedance measurements. The device under test (DUT) is connected in shunt between the signal line of a 2-port network. $S_{21}$ is measured and related to the DUT impedance $Z_x$:

$$S_{21} = \frac{2 Z_x}{2 Z_x + Z_0} \implies Z_x \approx \frac{Z_0}{2} S_{21} \quad (\text{for } |Z_x| \ll Z_0)$$

Where $Z_0 = 50\,\Omega$. By taking the imaginary part of $Z_x$ and sweeping beyond the SRF:

$$ESL = \frac{\text{Im}(Z_x)}{\omega} = \frac{Z_0 \cdot \text{Im}(S_{21})}{2 \omega}$$
Primary Limitation: Coaxial ground loops. Common-mode currents flowing on the shield bypass the DUT, leading to severe underestimations of impedance and overestimations of ESL without isolation transformers.

4. RF Current-Voltage (RF I-V)

RF Impedance Analyzer

Core Governing Physics:

A dedicated high-frequency impedance extraction technology. Unlike the ABB, it utilizes an RF directional coupler or a calibrated voltage-current ratio divider within an RF test-head adapter.

$$I_{\text{dut}} = \frac{V_i - V_r}{Z_0}, \quad V_{\text{dut}} = V_i + V_r \implies Z_x = Z_0 \frac{V_i + V_r}{V_i - V_r}$$

Vector mathematics dynamically subtract the residual test-head contact impedance profile using standard Open/Short/Load calibration arrays.

Primary Limitation: Manual terminal placement shifts the current-loop physical geometry, leading to systematic calibration degradation.

5. Resonant Cavity / Perturbation

Microwave Cavity

Core Governing Physics:

The capacitor is inserted into a highly tuned hollow microwave cavity. The dielectric and conductive structures of the capacitor perturb the electromagnetic boundary conditions of the cavity's dominant TE/TM modes.

$$\frac{\Delta f}{f_0} \approx \frac{\int_{V} ( \Delta\epsilon \mathbf{E}_0 \cdot \mathbf{E} + \Delta\mu \mathbf{H}_0 \cdot \mathbf{H} ) dV}{2 \int_{V} ( \epsilon \mathbf{E}_0^2 + \mu \mathbf{H}_0^2 ) dV}$$

The shift in the resonant center frequency ($\Delta f$) and the widening of the half-power bandwidth (Q-factor degradation) are processed via electromagnetic modeling to back-calculate $ESL$.

Primary Limitation: High structural sensitivity; physical alignment and thermal cavity drift inject substantial metrological jitter.

6. Time Domain Reflectometry (TDR)

TDR Scope

Core Governing Physics:

An ultra-fast sub-nanosecond rise-time step generator injects a voltage wave down a highly controlled $50\,\Omega$ transmission line terminated by the shunt DUT.

The reflected voltage waveform $v_{\text{refl}}(t)$ is captured. Integrating the inductive reflection transient yields the localized inductance:

$$ESL = \frac{Z_0}{2} \int_{t_{\text{start}}}^{t_{\text{end}}} \left[ \frac{v_{\text{refl}}(t) - v_{\text{ideal}}(t)}{V_{\text{incident}}} \right] dt$$
Primary Limitation: Finite rise-time of the step generator ($t_r \approx 30\text{ ps}$ to $200\text{ ps}$) limits spatial separation of the terminal contact from internal foil structures.

7. Constant di/dt Pulse Method

Power Transient

Core Governing Physics:

Forces a linear current ramp ($i(t) = a \cdot t$) with high derivative ($di/dt \approx 100\text{ A/}\mu\text{s}$ to $1000\text{ A/}\mu\text{s}$) directly through the capacitor.

The voltage across the capacitor terminals is:

$$v_c(t) = R_{\text{ESR}} \cdot i(t) + L_{\text{ESL}} \cdot \frac{di}{dt} + \frac{1}{C}\int i(t)dt$$

At the immediate onset ($t \to 0^+$), current and capacitor charge are negligible ($i(0) \approx 0$ and $\int i \, dt \approx 0$). The equation simplifies directly to a step voltage:

$$v_c(0^+) = L_{\text{ESL}} \cdot \frac{di}{dt} \implies L_{\text{ESL}} \approx \frac{\Delta V(0^+)}{di/dt}$$
Primary Limitation: High-speed parasitics of the current source (diode reverse recovery, switch parasitics) introduce ringing artifacts that mask the true $\Delta V(0^+)$.

Blueprint #1: VNA 2-Port Shunt-Thru (Gold Standard for RF/AC Sweep)

The VNA 2-Port Shunt-Thru is the absolute gold standard for frequency-domain ESL verification. By routing a $50\,\Omega$ port stimulus past the capacitor, we can measure high-frequency impedance without the error injected by probe contact impedance.

ASCII Schematic Representation:

Port 1                                                     Port 2
(V1, 50 Ohm)                                            (Rload, 50 Ohm)
  [+]----[ R_port1 ]----+-----------------------+-------[ R_port2 ]----[+]
         (50 Ohm)       |                       |       (50 Ohm)
                        |                       |
                        +-----------+-----------+
                                    |
                                  [ C_x ] (DUT Capacitance)
                                    |
                                  [ R_esr ] (DUT ESR)
                                    |
                                  [ L_esl ] (DUT ESL)
                                    |
                                   === GND

R_port1 port1 node_x 50
R_port2 port2 0 50

.subckt C_x_with_parasitics in out_gnd
C_internal in node_1 {C_x}

.ends

.ac dec 100 1k 1G

Mathematical Waveform Extraction:

In LTspice, run the AC sweep. To isolate the ESL, calculate the complex impedance $Z_x$ from the $S_{21}$ transfer function, then plot the imaginary part divided by $\omega$:

; Plot Impedance Magnitude: Z_mag = Mag(V(node_x) / I(R_port2)) ; Direct ESL Extraction Trace Formula: ESL_calc = Im(V(node_x)/I(R_port2))/(2*pi*f)

Blueprint #2: TDK Low Voltage Surge Discharge (Physical Ringing Method)

This time-domain method captures the natural resonant frequency of the closed-loop system during a high-speed discharge. By probing the capacitor terminals, we can de-embed fixture inductance via the ratio of initial voltage to inductive drop.

Mathematical Waveform Extraction:

Let $V_c$ be the initial charge voltage, $V_1$ be the immediate voltage step drop across the capacitor terminal, $T$ be the ringing period, and $I_{p1}, I_{p2}$ be consecutive current peaks.

; Extract Total Loop Inductance (L_total = L_fixture + L_esl): alpha = (1/T)*ln(Ip1/Ip2) omega_0 = sqrt((2*pi/T)**2 + alpha**2) L_total = 1 / (omega_0**2 * C) ; Extract Capacitor ESL (L_esl) directly from the voltage step ratio: L_esl = L_total * (V(v_cap_term)_at_step / Vc)
