Based on the research provided, the following integrated circuits (ICs) and specialized components are suitable for high-speed film capacitor charge-discharge circuits, categorized by their application to GaN and SiC technologies.

### 1. Integrated Solutions and Drivers for GaN FETs
For low-inductance applications (typically <650 V) such as ESL extraction, these ICs minimize gate loop parasitics to enable sub-nanosecond edges.

*   **EPC21701 / EPC21601 / EPC21603:** These are **integrated FET-plus-driver ICs** (eToF series). They collapse the gate loop into the package to achieve sub-2 ns switching and sub-1 ns voltage edges while eliminating gate-bounce artifacts.
*   **LMG1020 / LMG1025-Q1:** A Texas Instruments ultra-fast low-side driver capable of **1 ns minimum pulse widths**. It offers 7-A peak source and 5-A peak sink currents in a wafer-level chip-scale package (WCSP) to minimize inductance.
*   **NCP51820:** An onsemi high-speed half-bridge driver optimized for GaN. It features a **200 V/ns dV/dt rating** and matched propagation delays to ensure stable high-speed switching.
*   **LMG1210 / LM5113:** Half-bridge drivers often used as level shifters in common cathode configurations to drive high-side GaN FETs in pulse widths of 4–5 ns.

### 2. Specialized Drivers for Gate-Boosted SiC MOSFETs
For high-voltage setups (>1.2 kV), standard drivers are often too slow. The following ICs are used in research to implement the "gate-boosting" technique required for nanosecond transitions.

*   **DEIC420 (IXYS-RF Division):** An ultra-fast MOSFET driver in a low-inductance RF strip-line package. It features a **20 A peak output current** and is rated for 45 MHz, making it suitable for aggressive pulsed operation of high-voltage SiC and IGBT switches.
*   **Semikron Drivers:** These are utilized in high-accuracy pulse generators for their high voltage rating for input-to-output isolation and ability to drive power semiconductors directly.
*   **GaN FETs as Driver Switches (e.g., GS61004B / GS66508T):** In advanced "super-boosting" circuits, GaN FETs themselves are used as the output stage of the driver to switch the gate of a larger SiC MOSFET. This configuration can achieve slew rates exceeding **1 kV/ns**.

### 3. Supporting Components for Pulse Sharpening
If your specifications require picosecond-scale edges (similar to mercury switches but without the jitter), these components are used to sharpen the pulses generated by the primary ICs.

*   **FMMT413 / FMMT417:** These are **Avalanche Transistors** used in Marx-bank driver stages to provide sub-nanosecond trigger pulses (down to 425 ps) for subsequent GaN or SiC stages.
*   **SAS (Silicon Avalanche Shapers):** Specialized diodes used as peaking switches to sharpen pulse edges to **50–200 ps**.

### 4. High-Power Module Integrated Drivers
For industrial-scale energy discharge (magnetic forming or rail guns), ABB provides complete modules where the driver is integrated directly into the housing of high-voltage thyristors or IGCTs.

*   **5SPY 36L4506 / 5SPR 26L4506:** These are 4.5 kV discharge devices that include **integrated driver units** optimized for fast switch-on with high current rise rates (up to 50 kA/µs).

### Recommended DUTs (Devices Under Test)
If you are also selecting the primary switches to be driven by the ICs above:
*   **GaN FETs:** EPC2252 (80 V, 75 A), EPC2218A (80 V, 135 A), or EPC2034C (200 V, 213 A).
*   **SiC MOSFETs:** **C2M0080170P** is identified as one of the best-performing devices for ultrafast switching (sub-2 ns) when paired with a gate-boosting driver.
